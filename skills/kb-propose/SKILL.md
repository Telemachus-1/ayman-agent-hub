# Propose a knowledge base entry

The knowledge base is a Notion database (link in `AGENTS.md`). Agents read only **Approved** entries. Ayman approves proposals in one reply.

## What belongs

Durable company knowledge: terms, business units, systems, teams, processes, how things work. **Never** personal matters, secrets, or anything about Lush Lobster.

## Steps

1. Search the knowledge base for the term first. If an Approved entry exists and is right, stop.
2. Create one entry with:
   - **Title**: the term or topic.
   - **Status**: Proposed.
   - **Body**: two to six plain sentences.
   - **Source**: where you learned it (doc, Linear card, Slack link, Granola meeting, Helm record).
   - **Why**: one line on why agents need it.
   - **Replaces**: the Approved entry it would change, if any. Never edit the Approved entry itself.
3. List your proposal in the daily update under "KB proposals", numbered, so Ayman can reply "approve 1, 3; reject 2".
