# Research and documents

*Source: Arthur's BU lead playbook v6.1 §3–4, adapted for Ayman.*

## What this covers

- Research from public sources and our own systems: suppliers, brands, markets, competitors, domains, prices.
- Documents: briefs, summaries, price comparisons, supplier lists, meeting notes, one-page proposals.
- Data look-ups: customers, orders, invoices, stock, products, supplier terms (see `helm-work`).

## Steps

1. **Ask first.** Confirm the question, the BU it's for, and what the answer will be used for.
2. **Start from what we already know:**
   - the knowledge base (Approved entries);
   - Granola meeting notes and transcripts;
   - Gmail threads, Slack, Linear, Google Drive and Helm.
   Check whether the supplier or product already exists in another BU.
3. **Then go outside:** public web sources. Prefer primary sources (company sites, registries, official documents).
4. **Write the answer as a document**, never only in chat:
   - lead with the answer;
   - cite a source for each claim;
   - give a **"Could not check"** section listing what you couldn't verify.
5. **Put it where it lives:** on the Linear card (as a Linear document or an attachment) or in the folder Ayman names. Link it.
6. **If you have to ask someone,** say where you already looked: `Checked: <where> — not found`.
7. **Propose knowledge base entries** for durable company facts you learned, with the `kb-propose` skill.

## Limits

- Never spend money (no domain purchases, no paid reports) without Ayman's yes.
- Never contact anyone to get an answer. Research only.
- Respect **Internal only** knowledge base entries: they never appear in anything meant for outside the company.
- Personal data only where the job needs it. Never anything under someone else's NDA.

## Good asks this skill handles (examples from the playbook)

- "Find ten UK distributors for this brand, with contact pages, and say which already supply us."
- "Compare the prices on this supplier's list with what we pay today, and show the biggest differences."
- "Summarise my last three calls with this supplier from the meeting notes, with the actions we agreed."
- "List every open order for this customer, with its status and where it is now."
- "Check whether these domain names are free, and what each one costs to register." (Ask before anyone buys one.)
- "Write a one-page proposal for adding this product range, with margins at three price points."
- "Find every invoice over 30 days overdue in my BU, with the customer contact for each."
- "Check this new supplier's company record, website and public reviews, and flag anything that worries you."
- "Turn this spreadsheet of products into a clean list, and mark any that already exist in another BU."
