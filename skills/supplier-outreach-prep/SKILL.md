# Supplier and customer outreach prep

*Source: Arthur's BU lead playbook v6.1 §2–3 and §6, plus the knowledge base strategy entries.*

## Hard limits

- **Never send.** Not email, not Slack, not a form, not a call booking. Ayman, or the owner, sends.
- **Draft only when Ayman asks for a draft**, for that message. Drafting replies is not switched on by default (7 Nov 2026).
- Never commit Ayman or the company to anything: prices, quantities, dates, terms.
- Never contact a customer or supplier on your own initiative.

## Steps

1. **Ask first:** who, which BU, what Ayman wants out of the contact.
2. **Read before you write:**
   - search Gmail for every existing thread with that company or person;
   - check Granola for past calls, Linear for related cards, and Helm for existing orders, POs or terms;
   - check whether another BU already has the relationship. If so, say so before anything else.
3. **Research them** (see `research-brief`): company record, website, what they sell, who the right contact is.
4. **Prepare the points:** what we want, what we can offer, open questions, and what's already been said in past threads.
5. **If Ayman asks for a draft:**
   - draft from the mailbox that already holds the thread with that person. Never use the Telemachus mailbox for another BU's business;
   - show every recipient as `Full Name <address>`, never a bare address;
   - use the supplier-facing name for the BU (for example **Valen Trading** with suppliers, **Tallon Trading** with convenience wholesale customers; see the knowledge base);
   - leave it as a draft and tell Ayman where it is.

## What never goes in outreach

- Anything from an **Internal only** knowledge base entry: company IDs and tax numbers, payment entities, strategy.
- **The short-term Amazon-seller play is never mentioned to suppliers.** With suppliers we talk about the long-term play: helping them reach retailers they don't have access to today.
